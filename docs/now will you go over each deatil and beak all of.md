### **Executive Summary & Strategic Intent**

This project details the development of a multi-faceted property centered around a

**3,000 sq ft, 150-seat performance venue** designed to be completely self-sufficient through integrated biological systems1. The core of this endeavor is not simply to construct a building, but to serve as a

**focused research and development initiative**2. The central mission is to close the significant technological gap in emerging bio-energy technologies, specifically aiming to achieve power densities for Microbial Fuel Cells (MFCs) and Bio-Photoelectrochemical (BPEC) systems that are

**10-100 times beyond currently proven technology**3.

The long-term vision extends beyond the arts. The goal is to develop, patent, and perfect these integrated systems to create autonomous, self-sufficient energy and food solutions for residential applications, from tiny houses to suburban homes4. The theater project serves as the incubator and proof-of-concept for this potentially industry-altering technology5.

### **Core Infrastructure Systems**

The facility is supported by robust and redundant infrastructure designed for self-sufficiency and resilience.

#### **Electrical & Energy Systems**

The energy infrastructure is a hybrid system designed to generate, store, and efficiently use power from multiple renewable sources.

* **Main Service**: The grid connection will be a **400-amp, 240/120-volt single-phase service**19.

* **Distribution**: A **hub-and-spoke configuration** is used, with the main house acting as the central distribution point for monitoring and control20. Each major building cluster has its own electrical subpanel21.

* **Solar Power**: This is the primary renewable source with a total capacity of **150kW**22.

  * A  
    **120kW rooftop-mounted array** is distributed across the performance venue, main house, and hydroponics facility23.

  * A  
    **30kW ground-mounted array** provides additional capacity and serves as an educational demonstration24.

* **Wind Power**: A **10kW small wind turbine** on an 80-foot tower supplements solar generation25.

* **Geothermal System**: Ground source heat pumps provide efficient heating and cooling26.

  * Main House:  
    **5-ton** capacity system with vertical loops27.

  * Performance Venue:  
    **10-ton** capacity system with horizontal loops under the parking area28.

  * Artist Residences: Individual  
    **2-ton** systems with shared loop fields29.

  * Hydroponics: A dedicated geothermal system provides both heating and cooling30.

* **Energy Storage**: A **200kWh lithium iron phosphate (LFP)** battery system provides safe, long-lasting energy storage31. Its modular design allows for future expansion32.

* **Power Management**: A sophisticated system with automatic transfer switches manages the seamless transition between grid power, battery backup, and fully "islanded" operation during outages, prioritizing critical loads33.

#### **Water Management Systems**

The water system is designed with redundancy to serve both domestic and high-capacity agricultural needs.

* **Wells**: Two wells are planned34.

  * A  
    **domestic well** capable of producing **20 gallons per minute (GPM)**35.

  * An  
    **agricultural well** with a higher capacity of **50 GPM**36.

* **Storage**:  
  * A  
    **5,000-gallon** buried cistern for domestic water37.

  * A  
    **10,000-gallon** cistern for agricultural water, with both heated and unheated sections38.

#### **Wastewater Management Systems**

The system is designed to handle a peak event occupancy of

**350 people** while maximizing nutrient recovery and protecting groundwater39.

* **Main Treatment**: The main house and performance venue are served by a **3,000-gallon concrete septic tank** followed by an advanced treatment unit and a **constructed wetland** for final polishing40.

* **Residences**:  
  * **Composting toilets** are used to reduce the load on the main septic system41.

  * **Greywater wetlands** treat water from sinks and showers, with the recycled water used for landscape irrigation42.

* **Agricultural Wastewater**: Drainage from the hydroponics systems will receive specialized treatment to manage its nutrient-rich content43.
