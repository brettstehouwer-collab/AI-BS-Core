To create an "out-of-the-box" software suite for Tony Noto and **Noto's Old World Italian Dining** (a legendary staple in Grand Rapids, Michigan), you have to look past everyday restaurant software. He already has a Point-of-Sale (POS) system, a reservation app, and a website.

To win a pitch using your **AI-BS (Artificial Intelligence Business Solutions/Systems)** brand, you have to understand exactly who Tony Noto is. He isn’t just a restaurant owner; he is a former business and marketing educator (East Kentwood High School & Ferris State), a presenter on the "Art of Fine Dining and Business Etiquette," a massive community figure, and an advocate for Bocce ball (former chairperson for the Meijer State Games). Furthermore, Noto's operates a world-class subterranean wine cellar and massive banquet facilities.

To pitch AI-BS to him, you need applications that bridge **"Old World Tradition with New World Intelligence."** You want to sell him software that doesn't replace the human touch, but *scales his family's legacy.*

Here is a blueprint for 5 highly customized, out-of-the-box AI applications you could build under your AI-BS umbrella tailored specifically to Noto's:

### **1\. The "Tony Noto AI Etiquette Simulator" (Staff Training)**

* **The Noto Context:** Tony taught business, marketing, and etiquette for 25 years. He has incredibly high standards for how guests should be treated, but as a busy owner, he cannot personally train every single new busboy and server.  
* **The AI-BS App:** A voice-interactive Large Language Model (LLM) training app for new hires. Before a new server is allowed on the dining room floor, they have to role-play with the AI-BS simulator on an iPad. The AI verbally plays the role of a difficult customer, an allergy-prone diner, or a VIP asking complex questions about the Veal Saltimbocca.  
* **The Value:** The AI evaluates the server's tone of voice, empathy, and menu knowledge, scoring them based purely on *Tony Noto's specific etiquette curriculum*. It digitizes his teaching legacy.

### **2\. "Bocce-Vision" & The Upsell Engine**

* **The Noto Context:** Tony has deep roots in the community and was the Bocce chairperson for Festa Italiana for almost 20 years.  
* **The AI-BS App:** You install a localized, AI-powered camera system (or an augmented reality mobile app feature) for Noto's bocce courts. Using **Computer Vision**, the AI instantly measures the distance of all bocce balls to the *pallino* down to the millimeter, acting as a digital referee and eliminating arguments over who is closer.  
* **The Value:** It gamifies the experience. The AI tracks a live leaderboard displayed on TVs near the bar. The AI-BS system tracks game time and sends automated SMS prompts: *"Your game is heating up\! Tap here to have a server bring two Peronis and an appetizer right to the court."*

### **3\. The Generative AI "Banquet Architect"**

* **The Noto Context:** Noto’s makes a massive chunk of its revenue from weddings and corporate events across its various spaces (which can hold hundreds of guests).  
* **The AI-BS App:** An AI-driven event visualization tool for his event coordinators. A bride walks in and says, *"I want a romantic, candle-lit wedding in this room with Tuscan-style farm tables, sage green linens, and white roses."*  
* **The Value:** The planner types this prompt into the app. Using a custom image-generation AI (trained on photos of Noto's actual empty banquet rooms), the app instantly generates a photorealistic 3D mockup of the room decorated *exactly* as she described. It turns a 3-week back-and-forth quoting process into an instant "Wow\!" moment that locks in $20,000 event deposits on the spot.

### **4\. The "Cellar Master" AI Sommelier & Palate Profiler**

* **The Noto Context:** Noto’s is famous for its massive wine cellar. However, a giant wine list intimidates 95% of guests, leaving them to order a basic house red because they are afraid of making a mistake.  
* **The AI-BS App:** Instead of a digital PDF, guests access the AI-BS Cellar Master via a QR code. They take a fun, 3-question psychological flavor quiz (e.g., *"Do you prefer the smell of a campfire or fresh rain?"*).  
* **The Value:** The AI cross-references their flavor profile with their dinner order and searches Noto’s *live inventory* to recommend three specific wines at different price points. On the backend, it alerts Tony when specific vintages downstairs have reached their peak drinking age so the algorithm can actively push them to guests, moving stagnant inventory.

### **5\. Plate-Scraping AI (Waste & Portion Optimizer)**

* **The Noto Context:** In a massive operation that handles fine dining and massive buffets/banquets, food waste eats profit margins alive.  
* **The AI-BS App:** A small computer-vision camera mounted over the dishwashing "scrape" station in the back-of-house. As plates come back from the dining room, the AI-BS model scans them.  
* **The Value:** It aggregates the data and gives Tony's Executive Chef a weekly dashboard: *"Guests are consistently leaving 30% of the side pasta on the plate, but finishing 100% of the steak. Reduce the pasta portion by 2 ounces."* This invisible AI saves thousands of dollars a month in food costs without the guest ever noticing a difference.

Tony is a highly educated businessman. He will see right through a gimmicky tech pitch. When you package this up, do not sell him "restaurant software." Sell him an **"Operating System for Hospitality."**

I want to deploy a Generative AI Banquet Architect that allows brides to visually design their wedding in your halls in real-time. AI-BS takes cutting-edge intelligence and uses it to protect and scale your family's Old World legacy."*
