# Julie's Place: Complete 3D Asset Inventory

This is your master checklist for building the **full** "Julie's Place" world in Unreal Engine 5. 

While the Python script spawned the core structural proxies (the 15 main anchors), creating a photorealistic, cinematic world requires dressing the sets with secondary props, textures, and lighting. 

You can find the vast majority of these for **free** in Quixel Bridge (Megascans), with vehicles and specialty props available on the Unreal Engine Marketplace.

---

## 📍 SCENE 1: The Gated Mansion Estate (Exterior)
*The grand establishing shot of the Stehouwer family wealth.*

### Structural & Proxy Replacements
- [ ] **Mansion Facade** *(Proxy: Julie_Mansion_Structure)* - Large modular mansion walls, pillars, and a grand roof.
- [ ] **Swan Fountain** *(Proxy: Swan_Water_Fountain)* - A large stone water feature for the circular driveway.
- [ ] **Kirt's Gold SUV** *(Proxy: Gold_SUV)*
- [ ] **Julie's Porsche** *(Proxy: Porsche_Convertible)*
- [ ] **Mini Rolls Royce** *(Proxy: Mini_Rolls_Royce_Kid)* - The kids' drivable toy car.

### Landscaping & Set Dressing (Quixel)
- [ ] **Driveway Surface:** Asphalt, cobblestone, or gravel textures.
- [ ] **Lawn & Foliage:** High-quality grass (Nanite), manicured hedges, and large oak or palm trees.
- [ ] **Security:** Wrought iron estate gates and brick perimeter walls.
- [ ] **Lighting:** Fancy exterior street lamps or pathway lights.
