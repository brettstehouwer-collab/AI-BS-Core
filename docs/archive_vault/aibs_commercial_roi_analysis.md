## 1. 📊 Market Demand & Unique Value Proposition

### The Problem in Current AI Platforms:
- Platforms like Midjourney, Runway, Leonardo, and OpenAI enforce **$30 to $95/month recurring subscriptions**.
- Indie creators, freelancers, students, and small developers often only need AI compute for a single project (1-2 days) and do not want to be trapped in monthly recurring charges.

### Your Unique Advantage:
- **$4.99 24-Hour Day Pass:** Grants 250 AI generations (150 images + 5 videos + unlimited chat) for 24 hours without any monthly commitment.
- **OpenAI-Compatible REST API:** Developers can drop your API endpoint into existing apps (LangChain, AutoGen, Next.js, Python scripts) simply by changing `base_url`.
