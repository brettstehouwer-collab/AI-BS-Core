# The Bad Side Upside Down - Video Diffusion Prompts

Here are the highly descriptive visual prompts optimized for LTX-Video generation, mapped directly from the 5 scenes in `BUDMD.md`. Because diffusion models (like LTX-Video and Sora) cannot generate dialogue or complex audio, these prompts focus entirely on lighting, cinematic camera movement, character appearance, and surreal aesthetics.
