## 4. Low-Value / Toxic Ideas from the Zip (Objective Rejection)

The following items in `Untitleddocument.html` were analyzed and are **strictly recommended against**:

1. **Bandwidth Sharing (Honeygain, Pawns.app, PacketStream):**
   - **Claims:** "Make $20–$50/month sharing unused internet."
   - **Empirical Reality:** Yields only $5 to $12 per month on a residential connection.
   - **Severe Risks:** These services sell your residential IP address to third-party proxy networks. Abusers frequently use residential proxies for web scraping, botting, and ad fraud. This causes your home IP address to be blacklisted across Cloudflare, Google, and banking portals, forcing constant CAPTCHAs on household devices and risking ISP service termination.
   - **Verdict:** **REJECT.** The marginal return (~$0.25/day) does not justify the IP degradation risk.

2. **Tracking & Survey Panels (Nielsen Computer Panel, SavvyConnect):**
   - **Claims:** "Earn cash rewards in the background."
   - **Empirical Reality:** Pays $2 to $5/month in gift card points.
   - **Severe Risks:** Requires installing root certificates or persistent browser extensions that inspect, decrypt, and log web browsing telemetry and URLs. Degrades browser performance and introduces severe data leakage risks.
   - **Verdict:** **REJECT.**
