## Suggested portal organization

1. **Today / follow-ups:** explicit replies and requested calls, staff owner, next action and due date. Click-only observations stay separate from confirmed interest.
2. **Families & athletes:** linked but separate parent/guardian and athlete records; source, cohort, contact role, permission, opt-outs, call notes and corrections. Keep coaches and organizations separate.
3. **Messages & approvals:** draft versions, recipient preview, Dennis approval, exclusions, schedule and durable job status. Preparing or approving a draft must not itself send it.
4. **Registrations & services:** free registration, consultation, offer selected, payment state and promised deliverables. These stages are recommendations; current external signup/payment connections are not established by this review.
5. **Media & delivery:** footage intake, video edits, endorsement drafts, revision approval, delivery and completion records.
6. **Results & work log:** actual provider outcomes, verified replies, calls, registrations and sales alongside staff hours, expenses and tasks. Exclude personal financial/family conversation from operational notes.

Recommended flow: reviewed source → role/permission checked → exact message and audience approved → bounded outreach → recorded reply or separately labeled engagement observation → assigned follow-up → registration/consultation → optional package → fulfillment → follow-up.
