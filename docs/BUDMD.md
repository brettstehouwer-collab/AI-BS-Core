CHARACTERS  
**FREDY (15):** Black hair, brown eyes. Silky shorts and a baseball t-shirt. Sunglasses.  
**HENNERY (15):** Black hair, brown eyes. Red shirt with paper pad design. Carries a notepad.  
**FLO (15):** Blonde hair in a big bun, blue eyes. Red shorts, white shirt. Handheld mirror and lip gloss.  
**FRESHEY (15):** Brown hair, blue eyes. Rainbow sandals. Always carries a GQ magazine.  
**JESUS:** White robe, wounds in hands and feet. Represents love and salvation.  
**DISTURBED (15):** Fredy's friend. Messy hair, mismatched shoes. Always spaced out.  
**SATIN:** Red robe, long red hair and nails. Represents evil and temptation.  
**MR. PIMP:** Store owner who dresses like a pimp.

EXT. STOREFRONT \- MORNING

A vivid contrast: red fire flames on one side, white clouds and a white picket fence on the other. The store front is decorated with candy images. The characters move like WEEBLES WOBBLE FIGURES, wading when they walk.

FREDY stands outside holding a candy bar. SATIN and JESUS stand on either side of him.

MR. PIMP  
WTF?

Satin stomps, punching the air.

SATIN  
Excellent job, Fredy. What candy bar did you steal?

FREDY  
Snickers.

SATIN  
Fucken A right\!

JESUS  
> > > > > > (shaking head)  
What kind of language is that to teach a child?

SATIN  
My language. Remember, you kicked me out of heaven. No rules here.

JESUS  
Fredy, why did you steal the candy bar?

Fredy pauses in shock. He looks at the ground, then walks toward Satin.

FREDY  
Why my name is Fredy, Jesus? Like Fredy Krueger, I just stole a candy bar. Fuck\!

JESUS  
Fredy, if you keep stealing, you will be in jail one day.

FREDY  
I was thinking HELL, Jesus, but you're telling me to straighten up, so I have a chance?

JESUS  
Fredy, listen with your heart.

FREDY  
With my heart\! My heart is what leads to this mess\!

INT. KITCHEN \- EARLY MORNING

Information about Harris is spread across the table. HENRY and FLO drink coffee. FREDY walks in, hands raised.

FREDY  
Dam, I had one hell of a nightmare last night.

HENRY  
What you talking about last night?

FREDY  
Shit. What the hell is this shit on the table? Its trash for the garbage company to take out.

EXT. FRONT PORCH \- SATURDAY MORNING

It is raining. DISTURBED stands on the porch, umbrella held awkwardly. FREDY opens the front door.

FREDY  
Baden bullshit this morning, or just Disaster?

DISTURBED  
You're going to keep laughing or let me in\!

INT. MR. PIMP'S STORE \- LATE MORNING

The kids sit on benches drinking soda. FREDY paces in front of them like a professor.

FREDY  
This is recent gossip. Listen up\! South Park sets it above other shows with the quick production time. They can comment on a political event four days after it happens.

FRESHEY  
I don't think Beavis and Butthead were that funny, if at all humorous.

HENRY  
Subject to opinion. That crude commentary was a groundbreaking aspect of the show.

CUT TO:

EXT. PARK \- DAY

FREDY and DISTURBED prepare firecrackers.

FREDY  
See you at the park. Bring the works and a lighter\!
