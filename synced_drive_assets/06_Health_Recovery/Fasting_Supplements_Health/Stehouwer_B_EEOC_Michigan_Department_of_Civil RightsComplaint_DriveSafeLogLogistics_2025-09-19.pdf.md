**To Whom It May Concern:**

This letter serves as a formal complaint against Drive Safe Logistics LLC for creating, enabling, and participating in a hostile work environment. The events described below detail a systematic pattern of workplace mobbing, direct harassment by the company owner, a complete denial of due process, and retaliatory actions that continued even after my employment ended. These actions forced my constructive discharge and constitute a severe violation of my rights as an employee.

**1\. Chronology of Harassing Conduct**

I was re-hired by Drive Safe Logistics LLC in early September 2025\. I returned with the explicit intention of performing my duties professionally and positively. However, I was immediately met with a pre-existing toxic culture that devolved into a targeted campaign of harassment against me.

* **Initial Warning of Hostile Environment:** Shortly before the primary incident, I received an unsolicited text message from a coworker warning me, *"Just be careful it’s alot of rumors supposedly going around just stay to yourself and out of it."* This serves as direct, third-party proof that a coordinated, negative campaign was actively being waged against me without my knowledge.  
* **Direct Harassment by Company Owner (September 14, 2025):** At approximately 7:20 PM, I received a series of aggressive and defamatory text messages directly from the company owner, Jon McCormack. With no prior discussion, he falsely accused me of "creating unnecessary drama," "talk\[ing\] about peoples girlfriends," and warned me not to "approach any female that works at the station." He concluded by demanding I "apologize to Damian."  
* **Denial of Due Process and Refusal to Investigate:** I was completely blindsided by these unsubstantiated accusations. I immediately and repeatedly requested a phone call to understand the basis for these claims and be presented with any evidence. My requests were unequivocally denied. The owner stated, *"I'm not calling to discuss. As I said, I've already spoken about this longer than I've wanted to,"* effectively silencing any attempt on my part to engage in a fair process.  
* **Management Endorsement of Hostility and Constructive Discharge:** My attempts to explain my confusion were dismissed. When I expressed that I felt targeted, the owner's response was a clear ultimatum: *"If you feel that people are out to get you, you should find another job."* This statement constitutes a clear abdication of managerial responsibility and served as a direct endorsement of the hostile environment, making my continued employment untenable.  
* **Formal Resignation (September 18, 2025):** Left with no reasonable alternative, I formally resigned, citing in my message to management that my departure was a "constructive discharge, resulting from a hostile work environment and a persistent pattern of workplace mobbing."  
* **Post-Resignation Retaliation (September 19, 2025):** The harassment continued after my employment ended. I received an unsolicited, hostile, and retaliatory text message from a dispatcher, Damian Mcloud. Mr. Mcloud attacked my character with a barrage of insults, including calling me a "weak man," "sad lonely and a loser," and a "liar." This message serves as undeniable evidence of the pervasive and toxic culture and constitutes a clear act of retaliation after I had already removed myself from the situation.

**2\. Conclusion and Legal Claims**

The actions of Drive Safe Logistics LLC, led by its owner, created working conditions so intolerable that a reasonable person in my position would have been compelled to resign. This was not a voluntary separation but a **constructive discharge**. The owner's direct participation in the harassment, his refusal to investigate, and the post-resignation retaliation from another employee demonstrate a severe failure to maintain a safe work environment free from harassment.

It should be noted that the coworker who confirmed the rumors must remain anonymous out of a legitimate fear for their job security, as this palpable fear of retaliation is a clear indicator of the hostile work environment fostered by management.

Given the nature of these events, I am formally stating my concern about potential **retaliation** for filing this complaint. I request that my report be handled with the highest degree of professional discretion.

I am requesting a full investigation into these matters. I have retained all text message records as irrefutable evidence to substantiate these claims.
