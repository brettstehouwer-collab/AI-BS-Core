# Zero-Mock Real Money & Fiscal Tax Accounting Rule

**Status:** ACTIVE & IMMUTABLE DIRECTIVE  
**Classification:** Financial Accounting & Fiscal Compliance  

## Directives
1. **Zero-Mock Mandate:** NEVER implement, insert, seed, or display mock, sample, or placeholder financial transactions, cryptocurrency balances, or dollar figures across any financial, tax, or accounting modules (e.g. `crypto_transfers`, `accounting_entries`, `CryptoAccountingTab.jsx`, `MasterAccountingTab.jsx`, `aibs_master.db`).
2. **Fiscal Integrity Standard:** All ledger records, income figures, expense rows, and tax deductions MUST strictly originate from verified, real-world on-chain broadcasts, actual bank transactions, or explicit manual user submissions.
3. **Audit Compliance:** Never display synthetic numbers that could corrupt accounting, financial audits, or IRS Form 8949 / Schedule C reporting. If no live data is connected, render clean empty states ($0.00 / 0 records) rather than synthetic placeholders.
