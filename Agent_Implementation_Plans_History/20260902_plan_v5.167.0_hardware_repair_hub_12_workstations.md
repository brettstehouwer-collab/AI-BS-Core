# Hardware Master Repair Hub 12-Workstation Expansion Implementation Plan (v5.167.0)

## Overview
Implement 4 additional high-liability workstations into `PhoneRepairGuideTab.jsx`:
- **Station 9 (Part SKUs & Cross-Compatibility Finder):** Real-time compatibility lookup matrix across Apple iPhone, iPad, Android Phones, and Consoles.
- **Station 10 (DC Bench Power Supply & Waveform Diagnostic Lab):** 4-digit digital bench supply simulation with LED readouts (`V`, `A`, `W`), signature fault presets, oscilloscope curve analysis, and diode mode multimeter probe test points.
- **Station 11 (24-Point Asurion Audit & Printable QA Certificate):** 24-point pre/post IQC inspection checklist with 1-click Pass/Fail controls, customer & device metadata fields, and 1-click clipboard certificate generation.
- **Station 12 (Chemical Safety, MSDS & Thermal Runaway Containment):** Standard operating procedures for chemical solvents and emergency 4-step lithium-ion thermal runaway isolation protocol.

## Architecture
- Offline-first state initialization from `defaultPhoneRepairData.js`.
- Responsive workstation switcher ribbon in `PhoneRepairGuideTab.jsx`.
- 100% client-side fallback with zero runtime latency.
