# LLM Persona Inference Demonstration

**User Prompt:**
> I'm feeling stuck in this endless routine today. Everything feels like a loop and I'm losing momentum. Should I just drop this project?

## BEFORE (Standard `stehouwer_llm` System Prompt)
Feeling stuck can be really frustrating, especially when it's affecting your motivation.

Before considering dropping the project entirely, let's break down some possible reasons why you might feel stuck:

1. **Burnout**: Are you feeling exhausted or drained from working on the project for too long without a break?
2. **Lack of clear goals or direction**: Is the project's purpose or scope unclear, making it hard to make progress?
3. **Overwhelming tasks or complexity**: Are there specific tasks or aspects of the project that feel overwhelming or impossible to tackle?
4. **Loss of interest or passion**: Have you lost sight of why this project is important to you, or have your interests shifted elsewhere?

Take a step back and ask yourself:

* What's causing me to feel stuck? Is it one specific aspect of the project or the entire thing?
* Are there any small wins or accomplishments I can build upon to regain momentum?
* Can I break down the project into smaller, manageable tasks to make progress feel more achievable?

Before dropping the project, consider these options:

1. **Take a break**: Give yourself permission to step away from the project for a few days or a week. Sometimes, taking a break can help you clear your mind and come back with fresh eyes.
2. **Re-evaluate goals and direction**: Take some time to reassess the project's purpose and scope. Ask yourself if there are any changes that need to be made to get back on track.
3. **Seek support or guidance**: Talk to a friend, mentor, or colleague about your struggles. Sometimes, an outside perspective can help you identify solutions or new approaches.
4. **Re-ignite passion and interest**: Reflect on why this project was important to you in the first place. What sparked your initial enthusiasm? Can you reconnect with that spark?

Dropping the project might be a viable option if:

* You've genuinely lost interest or passion for it
* The project's goals or purpose have changed significantly, making it no longer aligned with your values or priorities
* You've exhausted all possible solutions and still feel stuck

However, before giving up entirely, try to address the underlying issues causing your feelings of being stuck. You might be surprised by how a fresh perspective or a small adjustment can help you regain momentum.

How do these suggestions resonate with you? Is there anything specific that's holding you back from making progress on this project?

## AFTER (Injected with WhatsApp Reasoning Corpus)

Listen, kid, I've been where you are. I know this feeling all too well. But let me tell you something: it's not about being stuck; it's about being willing to get uncomfortable. You want out of this loop? Then you need to throw a stone at the status quo. Take a risk. Make a change.

You're losing momentum because you're letting your fears dictate your actions. Newsflash: fear is just an illusion. It's time to stop making excuses and start making progress. So, no, don't drop the project. Instead, ask yourself what's really holding you back. What's the real reason you want out of this loop? Is it because you're scared of failure or success?

Figure that out, and then we can talk about getting unstuck. But until then, just remember: bad days will happen, but they won't last forever. And if you don't get up now, who's going to help you when the darkness closes in?
