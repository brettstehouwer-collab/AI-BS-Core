🚀 The 3-Step Studio Tracking Workflow

Follow these 3 core steps to record, process, and auto-transcribe vocal takes locked to the 80 BPM grid:

STEP 1: Load & Check Latency
  • Click Load October 7 Audio to initialize backing beat (80 BPM, C Minor).
  • Check the top RTL badge. Click it anytime to calibrate hardware loopback latency.
  • Click Enable Mic to grant microphone input access.
STEP 2: Arm Track & Record
  • Select your target track: Lead Vocal, Double (L), Double (R), or Harmonies and verify it is armed (REC).
  • Click Record Track (or press R). A 4-beat visual countdown pre-roll will engage.
  • Sing with the teleprompter while watching the 1.5s blue in-breath bar before each line.
STEP 3: Auto-Notes & Export
  • Click Record Track again to stop. The take automatically trims hardware RTL latency for sample-accurate phase lock!
  • The engine automatically runs pYIN pitch-to-MIDI note clustering into Auto-Notes (MIDI).
  • Review performance grades and export individual stems or the complete Master Mix WAV.

🎛️ Studio Vocal FX Rack Architecture

Real-time low-latency DSP processing chain engineered for high-impact vocal delivery:

1. Vocal EQ & High-Pass Filter

• 80Hz Low-Cut: Cuts subterranean desk rumble, HVAC hum, and mic proximity mud.
• Presence (3.2 kHz): Boosts vocal consonant articulation so words pierce through dense rhythm tracks.
• Air Sheen (10 kHz): Adds high-frequency silky polish mimicking vintage high-end tube microphones.

2. Studio Dynamic Compressor & Live GR Meter

• Glues whispered spoken verses and aggressive vocal screams into a tight, consistent dynamic pocket.
• Threshold (-20 dB) & 4:1 Ratio: Optimal for modern rock/pop vocal performance.
• Real-Time GR Meter: Shows exact decibels of gain reduction. Aim for -3 dB to -6 dB of reduction on peaks.

3. Tempo-Synced 80 BPM Stereo Delay

• Locks rhythmically to the song tempo (1/4 Note = 750ms, 1/8 Note = 375ms, 1/16 Note = 187.5ms).
• Features a dedicated 2.6kHz low-pass feedback damping filter to keep echoes warm without clashing with the lead vocal.

4. Algorithmic Convolution Reverb

• Plate: Dense, bright reflections tailored for upfront modern vocals.
• Hall: Expansive cathedral decay suited for emotional climaxes (Verse 3 & Outro).
• Chamber: Warm, intimate reflections that add body to spoken-word phrasing.

5. Real-Time Auto-Tune & Pitch Snap

• Scale-Locked Quantization: Automatically snaps vocals to E Minor / G Major (the core key of October 7) or Chromatic, C Minor, A Minor, D Minor, B Minor.
• Retune Speed (0 - 60ms): 0ms produces the iconic instantaneous modern snap; 20-40ms maintains natural human vibrato and micro-inflections.

6. Dynamic Vocal De-Esser & Sidechain Listen

• Isolates high-frequency sibilance (5.5 kHz – 8.5 kHz) using a dedicated bandpass sidechain detector.
• Automatically ducks harsh "s", "t", and "sh" consonant spikes with up to 18 dB of dynamic attenuation without dulling the vocal air.
• Check Audition Sibilance (Listen) to solo the detection band while adjusting the threshold.

7. Stereo Vocal Doubler & Micro-Pitch Detune

• Creates a wide, 3D stereo vocal image from a single take using Haas effect time offsets (12ms – 36ms).
• Integrates an active dual-phase LFO that detunes Left/Right channels by ±9 cents to simulate the organic thickness of two singers.

8. Vocal Formant & Throat Resonator

• Formant Shift (±15%): Morphs vocal tract length independently of pitch. Shift negative for deeper chest warmth, or positive for brighter, forward mask resonance.
• F1 & F2 Cavity Boost: Dial in pharyngeal throat body (F1) and oral palatal clarity (F2).

🎹 Vocal Auto-Notes & Piano Roll Transcriber

Automatic transcription of raw singing audio into discrete musical notes and MIDI files:

How Auto-Notes Works:

• The engine analyzes human vocal frequencies (65 Hz to 550 Hz) using the pYIN algorithm paired with median filtering.
• Vibrato flutter and vocal micro-tremors are smoothed into discrete musical notes locked to the 80 BPM grid.
• Transient noises shorter than 80ms are filtered out to prevent breath clicks from creating phantom notes.

Visual Piano Roll & Intonation Color Coding:

• Displays vertical pitch lanes from C2 (MIDI 36) to C5 (MIDI 72).
• 🟢 In-Tune: Pitch accuracy within ±12 cents of true C Minor root frequencies.
• 🔵 Flat: Pitch dropped more than 12 cents flat (needs lifting).
• 🔴 Sharp: Pitch exceeded 12 cents sharp (needs lowering).

Exporting to External DAWs (Ableton, FL Studio, Logic, Pro Tools):

• Click Export MIDI (.mid) to download a Type 0 standard MIDI file containing every sung note, duration, and velocity.
• Drag and drop October7_Vocal_AutoNotes.mid into any external synthesizer, vocal double synth, or notation software!

⚡ Windows Core Pro Audio & Hardware RTL Calibration

Under-the-hood Windows audio kernel optimizations that eliminate recording lag:

1. Windows MMCSS Pro Audio Elevation:

• Operating with Windows Administrator privileges enables the Multimedia Class Scheduler Service (MMCSS) via AvSetMmThreadCharacteristicsW("Pro Audio").
• Bypasses background Windows CPU throttling to prevent crackles, pops, and buffer underruns during live takes.

2. WASAPI Exclusive Hardware RTL Calibration:

• Clicking the top RTL badge triggers a hardware loopback probe measuring round-trip delay ($L_{\text{roundtrip}} = L_{\text{output}} + L_{\text{input}}$).
• Typical shared mode latency is 80–100ms; calibrated WASAPI exclusive mode achieves sample-accurate microsecond precision.

3. Automatic Sample-Accurate Take Alignment:

• When recording finishes, the DAW automatically trims the exact measured hardware latency samples from the start of the audio buffer.
• Your vocal transients lock 100% on the 80 BPM downbeats without needing manual nudge/slide editing!
